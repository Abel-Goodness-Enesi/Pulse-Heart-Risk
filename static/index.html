What this is and is not.

The model is a logistic regression on 14 measurements from the Framingham Heart Study, about 4,240 people from one town in Massachusetts. On held-out data it scores a PR-AUC of 0.300 against 0.152 for guessing. At the 0.15 line it catches about 59% of people who go on to develop heart disease, and about 26% of the people it flags do.

"About N of 100" is the model's probability read as a count. Calibration was not tested, so treat it as a ranking aid, not a forecast. The data is small and local, and the model says nothing about causes. This is a learning project and not medical advice.
