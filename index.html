Read this before you trust it

  • One town. Framingham, Massachusetts. Mid-century cohort; it may not describe you.
  • Small sample. 4,240 people, about 129 positives in test. A single person moves recall by ~0.8 points.
  • Ranks, doesn’t forecast. Calibration wasn’t tested, so “12 of 100” is a score, not a promise.
  • Learned association, not cause. Coefficients show what the model leans on, nothing more.
  • Rare inputs wobble. Prior stroke appears in ~0.4% of people; its weight rests on a handful of cases.

This is a portfolio demonstration of a machine-learning project. It is not medical advice and must not be used to make health decisions. Talk to a clinician.
